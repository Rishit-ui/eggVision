# Assembly Instructions

1. Prepare the enclosure (30x20x25cm).
2. Paint the interior matte black and let it dry completely.
3. Drill a hole at the top center for the LED wiring.
4. Mount the LED and attach the diffuser below it.
5. Secure the egg holder in the exact center of the base.
6. Cut a mounting hole for the camera (bottom or side) and attach it.
7. Mount the DHT22 sensor on an inside wall.
8. Route all wires through a small exit hole to the Raspberry Pi.
9. Connect wires according to the GPIO pinout.
10. Connect the camera ribbon or USB cable to the Pi.
11. Power the Pi and test the camera stream.
12. Test the LED lighting and verify the transillumination effect on an egg.
